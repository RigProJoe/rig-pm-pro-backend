## FILE NAMING CONVENTION

**All files must follow this naming standard:**
- ✅ lowercase letters only (no uppercase)

- ❌ NO underscores
- ❌ NO spaces
- ❌ NO UPPERCASE LETTERS

- ✅ `asset-crud-summary.md`

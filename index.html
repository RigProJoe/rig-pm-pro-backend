Keep your rig running. Keep your records straight.

Made for owner-operators who run their own rigs. Rig PM Pro is a web tool built for owner-operator truck drivers. Track preventive maintenance for your truck, APU, trailer, and reefer, log what you spent, and keep every receipt where you can find it.

Start your account See pricing
Works in any phone or computer browser. Nothing to install.

How it works

1

Add your equipment

Enter the make, model, and last 6 of the VIN for your truck, APU, trailer, or reefer. Don't see your make? Add it yourself.

2

Pick what to track

Choose the PM items you want to watch from a simple drop-down. Shorten any interval to suit your operation. It can't go past the manufacturer's interval.

3

Log work and receipts

Record completed PMs and repairs with date and cost. Snap a photo of a receipt from your phone and attach it.

Everything in one place

  • Asset pages for truck, trailer, APU, and reefer
  • Main PM screen that shows what's coming up
  • Costs totaled by month, quarter, year, and lifetime
  • Sort completed work by unit or asset type
  • Receipt upload from your phone's camera or photo library
  • Expense tracking with categories
  • Export your records for tax purposes
  • Monthly or annual billing
